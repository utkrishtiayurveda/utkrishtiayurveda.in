About the doctor

Care begins with understanding the person.

Dr. Shrashti Bhardwaj is the doctor behind Utkrishti Ayurveda. Her detailed professional introduction, education and clinical journey will be added after verification.

At the centre of this practice is a simple idea: a consultation should feel unhurried, clear and personal. The patient’s concerns, daily routine, food habits, sleep, stress and broader health context deserve to be understood together before care is planned.
